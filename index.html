Jeromy Chang

PhD Student in Computer Science and Engineering
Santa Clara University

I'm a PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering at Santa Clara University. I'm interested in making AI more reliable and trustworthy through better evaluation and a deeper understanding of model behavior. What excites me about AI research isn't just what these systems can do, but understanding how they behave, where they fall short, and why. My research interests span responsible AI, large language model evaluation, interpretability, and multilingual and multicultural NLP.

I'm particularly interested in how AI systems behave across different contexts, including languages, cultures, and evaluation settings, and how we can better identify, explain, and address their limitations. My research experience includes multilingual text generation, AI-generated text detection, and evaluating language model behavior. My research aims to help make AI systems not just more capable, but also more reliable, interpretable, and trustworthy for all of society.

GRADE: Evaluating AI Tutors

What makes an AI tutor's feedback genuinely useful? GRADE studies how open-source language models assess mistake identification, error location, guidance, and actionability—and how fine-tuning, reasoning, and data augmentation shape that evaluation.
